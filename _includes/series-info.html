{%- comment -%} Works out whether a post is part of a lab-notebook series, from its title. A series title looks like "Series Name: Post Name [2/7]". Sets: series_name (or nil), series_posts (oldest first), series_anchor. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign series_name = nil -%} {%- assign series_posts = nil -%} {%- assign si_title = include.post.title | default: "" -%} {%- if si_title contains ": " and si_title contains " [" -%} {%- assign si_tail = si_title | split: " [" | last -%} {%- if si_tail contains "/" and si_tail contains "]" -%} {%- assign series_name = si_title | split: ": " | first -%} {%- assign si_prefix = series_name | append: ": " -%} {%- assign series_posts = site.posts | where_exp: "p", "p.title contains si_prefix" | reverse -%} {%- assign series_anchor = series_name | slugify -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endif -%}

## Summary

Science Beyond Curriculum is my school's research-paper program, which I have taken part in across grades. My 2024 project, which I also wrote up for the Cambridge Science Competition, tested whether four low-cost, biodegradable adsorbents could improve water from Agara Lake in Bengaluru. It won Best Paper and Best Presentation.

Earlier SBC work includes two Grade 9 papers written in a team of four, a review of blood cancers and a paper on the RUNX1 gene's link to acute myeloid leukemia, and a [home wastewater treatment experiment](/projects/biology/2024/04/14/water-treatment.html).

## Methods

1. Used water samples from Agara Lake.
2. Prepared four adsorbents: crushed eggshells, coconut fiber, powdered orange peel and granular activated carbon.
3. Treated lake water samples with each adsorbent.
4. Measured pH, total dissolved solids (TDS) and electrical conductivity (EC) at 24 and 48 hours.

## Results and limitations

- Treatment for 24 hours outperformed 48 hours.

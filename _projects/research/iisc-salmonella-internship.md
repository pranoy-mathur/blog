## Summary

*Salmonella* has to survive harsh conditions in the gut before it can cause disease. I ran an independent project in Prof. Dipshikha Chakravortty's *Salmonella* pathogenesis lab on how its growth changes under three gut-adapted stresses: iron depletion, bile salts and acidic pH. I worked under a PhD student and also assisted PhD students across a range of wet-lab techniques, including DNA and RNA isolation, flow cytometry and cell culture.

I have written up the month in two posts: [what the internship was like](/biology/2025/05/12/IISc-internship.html) and [the experiment I ran at the end](/salmonella-growth-stress/).

I secured the position by cold-emailing the lab.

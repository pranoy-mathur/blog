**Purpose:** To measure cell growth over three days at different starting cell numbers using the MTT assay.

**Principle:**
Growth kinetics describes how fast cells multiply, for example through the population doubling time. One way to measure it is a proliferation assay in a 96-well plate.

The MTT assay shows whether cells are metabolically active. MTT is a yellow tetrazolium dye. In living cells, mitochondrial enzymes that use NAD(P)H convert MTT into purple formazan crystals. <!-- my notes say "NADPH dehydrogenase"; reworded to the general term --> If crystals form, the cells are respiring and are alive. The crystals are then dissolved in DMSO, and the absorbance is read in a spectrophotometer. Greater absorbance means more formazan, which means more viable cells. <!-- my notes say 545 nm; MTT is usually read at about 570 nm -->

The MTT assay is used to:
1. Test the cytotoxicity of substances such as drugs, nanoparticles and other compounds, and find the concentration at which a drug becomes toxic.
2. Measure cell growth.
3. Measure metabolic activity.
4. Assess responses to growth factors and other stimuli.
5. Screen drugs for their ability to promote cell growth.

**Protocol:**
*Plating*
1. I made a 2-fold serial dilution of cells across six tubes, C1 to C6. C1 started with 0.16 x 10^6 cells in 1400 µL of medium. I moved 700 µL from each tube into the next, which already held 700 µL of medium, and discarded the extra 700 µL from the last tube.
2. This gave 8000, 4000, 2000, 1000, 500 and 250 cells per well for C1 to C6. <!-- my notes say "cells/cm3"; I assumed per well -->
3. I added 100 µL from each tube to the plate in duplicate, with separate sets of wells for day 1, day 2 and day 3, plus control wells. Each tube had 600 µL for the wells plus 100 µL extra to allow for pipetting error.

*Reading*

4. On each reading day, I added MTT reagent to the wells and incubated them so the formazan crystals could form. <!-- added: this step is not in my notes; the usual method is MTT at 0.5 mg/mL for 3 to 4 hours at 37°C -->
5. I removed 100 µL of the supernatant. <!-- notebook could read 100 or 120 µL -->
6. I added 100 µL of DMSO to dissolve the crystals.
7. I incubated the plate at room temperature for 10 minutes.
8. I read the absorbance in the spectrophotometer.

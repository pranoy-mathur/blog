**Purpose:** To make a natural detergent from fruit peels to understand how enzymes break down organic matter.

**Principle:**
<!-- principle from Lab Report doc -->
Fats and oils in organic waste can be broken down by enzymes released by bacteria. The bacteria feed on the waste, and the result is a natural, safe cleaning product. A bio-enzyme detergent is biodegradable, has low toxicity and is not corrosive. The main enzymes in it are proteases, which break down proteins; lipases, which break down fats; and amylases, which break down starch.

Jaggery supplies sugar for the microbes to ferment, and the citrus peels supply the organic material they break down. <!-- added: role of jaggery and peels -->

**Materials:**
- Citrus fruit peels
- Jaggery
- Water
- A container that can be closed

**Protocol:**
<!-- protocol from Lab Report doc -->
1. I added 300 g of citrus fruit peels to the container.
2. I added 100 g of jaggery.
3. I added 1 liter of water.
4. I left it to incubate for 48 hours.

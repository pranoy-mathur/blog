**Purpose:** To test plant extracts for triterpenoids, polyphenols and alkaloids.

**Principle:**
Plants make primary metabolites, which take part directly in metabolism for growth and development, and secondary metabolites, which act as a first line of defense. Secondary metabolites are also called phytochemicals. Many have anticancer, anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, antibacterial or antiviral properties. The main types are saponins, terpenoids, polyphenols, flavonoids and alkaloids. Examples include curcumin from turmeric, which is a polyphenol, and resveratrol from grapes. <!-- added: my notes listed curcumin as a flavonoid; it is a curcuminoid, a type of polyphenol -->

Each test uses a reagent that reacts with one class of compound:
- **Salkowski test (triterpenoids):** the extract is mixed with chloroform, and concentrated sulfuric acid is added down the side of the tube. A reddish-brown color at the boundary between the layers shows triterpenoids. <!-- added: expected color -->
- **Folin-Ciocalteu (FC) test (polyphenols):** polyphenols reduce the FC reagent in alkaline conditions (from sodium carbonate), forming a blue color. <!-- added: chemistry of the FC reaction -->
- **Dragendorff's test (alkaloids):** alkaloids form an orange-brown precipitate with Dragendorff's reagent.

**Materials:**
- Extracts of cinnamon, turmeric, green tea and amaranthus leaves, and a bio-enzyme sample
- Chloroform and concentrated sulfuric acid
- Folin-Ciocalteu (FC) phenol reagent and sodium carbonate
- Dragendorff's reagent and dilute hydrochloric acid
- Test tubes and pipettes

**Protocol:**
<!-- protocol from Lab Report doc -->
*Triterpenoids (Salkowski test)*
1. I added 0.5 mL of the extract to a test tube.
2. I added 1 mL of chloroform.
3. I added a few drops of concentrated sulfuric acid.

*Polyphenols*

4. I added 1 mL of the sample to a test tube.
5. I added 500 µL of FC phenol reagent.
6. I added 500 µL of sodium carbonate.
7. I incubated the tube in the dark for 20 minutes and watched for a blue color.

*Alkaloids (Dragendorff's test)*

8. I added 1 mL of the sample to a test tube.
9. I added 1 mL of Dragendorff's reagent.
10. I added 0.2 mL of dilute hydrochloric acid.
11. I watched for an orange-brown precipitate.

**Purpose:** To thaw cryopreserved cells, remove the cryoprotectant and check how many cells survived.

**Principle:**
Cells can be stored frozen for a long time. Cells are kept in liquid nitrogen at -196°C, and tissues in -80°C freezers. Without protection, ice crystals form inside the cells and damage them. To prevent this, a cryoprotectant is added. The one used here is dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO), which enters the cells and replaces water molecules, so fewer ice crystals form. <!-- added: corrected name; my notes said "dimethyl sulphur oxide" -->

To use the cells again, they have to be brought back to 37°C. At 37°C, DMSO is toxic to cells, so thawing has to be fast, and the DMSO has to be removed quickly. The vial is warmed in a 37°C water bath only until it is about 70 to 80% thawed, with some ice crystals still left. Since a water bath is not sterile, the vial is wiped with 70% IPA before opening. The cells are then diluted in 10 mL of saline, which dilutes the DMSO, and centrifuged so the DMSO-containing liquid can be thrown away.

After thawing, the cells are counted and their viability is checked with trypan blue, which only enters dead cells. The viability should be above 90%. I did this with MDA-MB cells (a triple-negative breast cancer line) at passage 10.

**Protocol:**
1. I set the water bath to 37°C.
2. I noted the details on the vial label: cell line, passage number and number of cells.
3. I prepared a 15 mL Tarsons tube with 10 mL of sterile saline.
4. I thawed the vial quickly until it was about 70% thawed, with ice crystals still remaining.
5. I wiped the whole vial with IPA on a tissue and dried it with another tissue.
6. I transferred all the contents into the 15 mL tube.
7. I inverted the tube gently to mix.
8. I centrifuged it at 1500 rpm for 10 minutes.
9. I discarded the supernatant.
10. I added 1 mL of DMEM to the cell pellet.
11. I counted the cells and calculated the viability, using 10 µL of trypan blue with 10 µL of cells (a 2-fold dilution).

**Results:**
I thawed cells twice:

| Round | Total cell count | Live cell count | Viability |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (14 June) | 1.02 x 10^6 | 0.325 x 10^6 | 31.9% |
| 2 (28 June) | 1.05 x 10^6 | 0.37 x 10^6 | 35.2% |

Both rounds were below the 90% viability I was aiming for.

**Purpose:** To separate the components of plant leaf extracts using thin layer chromatography (TLC).

**Principle:**
<!-- principle from Lab Report doc -->
Chromatography has three stages: separation, identification and analysis. It uses two phases: a stationary phase that stays in place and a mobile phase that moves through it. The two phases must not mix, and usually one is lipophilic while the other is hydrophilic. In TLC, the stationary phase is a thin layer of silica gel on a plate, and the mobile phase is a solvent that moves up the plate.

Each component of the sample moves at a different speed depending on its polarity. Components with a high affinity for the stationary phase move slowly, while components that dissolve well in the mobile phase move further. Each component is described by its Rf value:

Rf = distance moved by the component / distance moved by the solvent from the origin

The Rf value depends on the solvent system, the amount of sample, the adsorbent and the temperature.

A related method, affinity chromatography, separates molecules by a specific binding interaction between an immobilized ligand and its binding partner. It is used to purify nucleic acids and proteins and in enzyme assays.

**Materials:**
- Silica gel
- Glass slide
- Oven
- Capillary tube
- Plant leaf extracts
- Butanol, acetic acid and water (4:1:5)
- Beaker

**Protocol:**
<!-- protocol from Lab Report doc -->
1. I mixed 5 g of silica gel with 10 mL of distilled water and spread it evenly on a glass slide.
2. I heated the slide in an oven at 105°C for 30 minutes.
3. I drew a starting line at one end of the plate.
4. I loaded the sample onto the line using a capillary tube.
5. I placed the plate in a beaker containing the mobile phase (butanol, acetic acid and water in a 4:1:5 ratio), with the starting line above the solvent level.
6. When the solvent had nearly reached the top, I removed the plate, marked the solvent front and let it dry. <!-- added: the doc's procedure stops at step 5 -->
7. I measured the distances and calculated the Rf value of each spot. <!-- added -->

**Purpose:** To isolate plasmid DNA from bacteria using the alkaline lysis method.

**Principle:**
Alkaline lysis separates small plasmids from the much larger bacterial chromosome using four solutions.

- **Solution I** (50 mM glucose, 10 mM EDTA, 25 mM Tris-HCl, pH 8.0): glucose keeps the osmotic balance, which stops the bacteria from bursting too early and helps prevent shearing of the DNA. EDTA chelates Ca2+ and Mg2+, which weakens the outer membrane and stops nucleases from working. Tris buffers the pH. <!-- EDTA and Tris roles from Lab Report doc -->
- **Solution II** (NaOH and SDS; 0.2 N NaOH and 1% SDS in the doc): SDS breaks down the cell membrane, so the cell contents are released. NaOH raises the pH, which denatures proteins, chromosomal DNA and plasmid DNA. The plasmid's two strands stay linked because it is a small closed circle, so its denaturation can be reversed. <!-- from Lab Report doc; wording simplified -->
- **Solution III** (potassium acetate; 3 M potassium acetate with 2 M acetic acid in the doc): this neutralizes the solution. The small plasmid quickly renatures and stays dissolved. The tangled chromosomal DNA, proteins and SDS form a white precipitate, since SDS turns into insoluble potassium dodecyl sulfate. Centrifuging removes the precipitate. <!-- from Lab Report doc -->
- **Solution IV** (ice-cold isopropanol, IPA): makes the plasmid DNA come out of solution so it can be pelleted. <!-- my notes say 70% ethanol and the doc says ice-cold IPA; IPA is used here because one volume of 70% ethanol is too dilute to precipitate DNA -->

**Protocol:**
1. I took 1.5 to 2 mL of bacterial culture.
2. I spun it down at 6000 rpm for 10 minutes at 4°C.
3. I discarded the supernatant and added 100 µL of Solution I.
4. I vortexed gently and incubated the tube on ice for 5 minutes.
5. I added 200 µL of Solution II and mixed by inverting the tube 5 to 10 times.
6. I added 150 µL of Solution III and mixed by inverting.
7. I placed the tube on ice for 5 minutes.
8. I spun it at 7000 rpm for 15 minutes at 4°C.
9. I collected the supernatant in a new tube and added 450 µL of Solution IV.
10. I incubated it for 10 to 15 minutes on ice.
11. I centrifuged it at 10,000 rpm for 20 minutes.
12. I decanted the supernatant and blot dried the pellet.
13. Once the pellet was dry, I added 20 µL of nuclease-free water.
